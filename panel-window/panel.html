Chat & flow

Context is attached only when you send. Hover a chat message for Edit or Branch. History is capped at 50 messages.

Focus & Reminders

Set custom focus, short-break and long-break minutes. Reminders have their own section with repeat, snooze, popup and alarm sound.

Privacy

Context, clipboard and screenshots are click- or send-only. Screenshot context is off by default. Redaction masks keys, tokens, passwords and emails. Never send blocks matching messages.

Settings

The Settings section and header gear live inside the panel. The welcome screen only appears on a fresh install.
